## Assembling PIR Panels

Check all parts and fit them together without glue.

Prepare the floor panel as shown in the images:

<div class="grid cards" markdown>
- ![Preparing the iDryer floor part](../../img/cabinet/0004.png)
- ![Preparing the floor part](../../img/cabinet/0005.png)
</div>

Alternatively, use the rotating insert from the file [iDryer\_set2.3mf](../../../CAD/v3/iDryer_set2.3mf). With this method, you don't need to bevel the floor part-just cut out the missing rectangle in the milled air duct. The offset from the edge of the floor panel should be 5 mm (groove depth in the back wall).

<div class="grid cards" markdown>
- ![iDryer](../../img/cabinet/0015.png)
</div>

You will need a spatula from the printed parts kit and heat-resistant sealant.

Apply sealant to the parts:

<div class="grid cards" markdown>
- ![Preparing iDryer parts for assembly](../../img/cabinet/0006.png)
</div>

Spread it using the spatula:

<div class="grid cards" markdown>
- ![Preparing iDryer parts for assembly](../../img/cabinet/0007.png)
</div>

Assemble the box:

<div class="grid cards" markdown>
- ![Preparing iDryer parts for assembly](../../img/cabinet/0008.png)
</div>

Tape all sides of the box with masking tape:

<div class="grid cards" markdown>
- ![Preparing iDryer parts for assembly](../../img/cabinet/0009.png)
</div>

Glue the cap and printed part:

<div class="grid cards" markdown>
- ![Preparing iDryer parts for assembly](../../img/cabinet/0010.png)
- ![Preparing iDryer parts for assembly](../../img/cabinet/0011.png)
- ![Preparing iDryer parts for assembly](../../img/cabinet/0012.png)
</div>

Allow the sealant to cure fully.
