# iDryer x4 Filament Dryer

## Features

* Number of spools: 4
* Drying temperature: up to 100°C
* Heater temperature control: yes
* Air temperature control in the chamber: yes
* Drying time: unlimited
* Forced ventilation: yes
* Filament weighing: yes
* Presets: yes
* Supported filament types: PLA, ABS, HIPS, PETG, SBS, Flex, PA6, PA12, PC, ABS, ASA, PP, POM, and others

!!! warning annotate "Important"

    All internal parts inside the enclosure must be printed with materials that can withstand heating to 100°C or higher.

    The air duct insulation shown in the x4 project is provided for reference. You may use it as is or insulate the air duct with any thermal insulation material. Failure to do so will result in slower heating and significantly increased power consumption!
