# PIR Enclosure

PIR boards are a cheap and accessible material for building the iDryer enclosure.

## Advantages

* Low cost
* Easy and simple to work with
* Decent appearance
* Among the lowest thermal conductivity among insulation materials
* No harmful emissions when heated

## Disadvantages

* Can deform from impact

Both the spool holders and the air duct with the heater are printed from ABS CF10.


!!! warning annotate "Important"

    All internal parts of the enclosure must be printed from materials that can withstand 100°C or higher.


## Fabrication (\~60 minutes)

* Review the images
* Print the cutting plan file
* Print the knife fixture file
* Mark the sheet
* Cut the parts
* Bevel edges where needed
* Glue the parts (use dedicated PIR panel adhesive foam or any polyurethane-based glue)
* Seal edges and joints with aluminum tape
* Print the hinges, attach them with thin double-sided tape to the body and door, and screw them in with 25-30 mm long, 4-5 mm diameter screws. This method has been tested and proven reliable.

!!! quote annotate "Download files"

    :material-download:</i> **[Knife Fixture.stp](../../../CAD/v1/knife_fixture.stp)** - Knife fixture

    :material-download:</i>** [Cutting Plan.pdf](../../../CAD/v1/cutting_plan.pdf)** - Cutting plan


!!! warning annotate "Important"

    Note! The rear wall is glued from two parts to optimize material usage.
