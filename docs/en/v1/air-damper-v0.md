## Final Version

The final version of the chamber ventilation damper. This model is designed for enclosures with a 30mm wall thickness. However, by adjusting the thickness of the "Diffuser" part, it can be adapted to any housing. Installation is simplified-only a hexagonal through-hole needs to be cut. The diffuser is printed with minimal infill, providing effective thermal insulation.
